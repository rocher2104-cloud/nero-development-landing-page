Privacy & compliance

Built for enterprise governance.

Privacy is load-bearing infrastructure here, not an afterthought. Every session arrives with a complete chain of consent and documentation.

01

Biometric releases

Written consent from all crew members covering gait, hand geometry, and facial incidentals. Archives available under MSA terms.

02

Property access releases

Homeowners sign filming releases before any session. Documentation provided and auditable per deployment.

03

Automated PII blurring

Every frame processed for faces, screens, text, and identifiable documents. Redaction logs delivered with each dataset.

04

Data licensing — exclusive or shared

Exclusive licensing available — we do not resell, repurpose, or retain copies. Shared licensing arrangements also available on request.

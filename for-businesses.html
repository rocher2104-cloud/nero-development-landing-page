How It Works

From site visit to payout.

    Site assessment

  2. 02

    Deployment

  3. 03

Common Questions

What businesses ask before hosting.

Does this change how my team works?

No. Capture systems are designed to record ongoing operations without requiring workers to change their tasks.

What happens to footage of my staff?

Handling, redaction and consent terms are set out in the collection agreement for your site.

How long does a typical deployment run?

Deployment length varies by program and is agreed in advance.

Can I see what data is captured at my site?

Access to captured data from your site is defined in your collection agreement.

How is compensation determined?

Compensation terms are set per site based on the collection program's scope and duration.

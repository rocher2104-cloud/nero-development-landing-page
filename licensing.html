Licensing

Three ways to access Nero data.

Instead of buying a static dataset, connect your training pipeline to the physical world under a commercial model matched to how your team builds.

Licensing tiers

01

Dataset License

Non-exclusive access to a Nero dataset, shared across multiple licensees.

  • Fixed dataset snapshot
  • Shared, non-exclusive access
  • Fastest path to training data

02

Exclusive Dataset

A collection program produced for one client with agreed exclusivity over the resulting data.

  • Custom collection program
  • Exclusive to one client
  • Specification set by client

03

Continuous Data

An ongoing stream of newly captured and processed real-world data to an agreed specification.

  • Ongoing delivery, not a one-time drop
  • Specification evolves with the program
  • Best fit for continuously training models

How They Differ

Compare the three models side by side.
